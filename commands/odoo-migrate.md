Build an addon/dependency inventory and identify official evidence for the database path. Do not run an upgrade or transmit a database without explicit confirmation. Present a short plan and process one addon at a time; after each addon report checks/results and ask whether to continue unless batch work was explicitly authorized. Distinguish official Odoo processes from community tools and mark unknowns as unverified.
